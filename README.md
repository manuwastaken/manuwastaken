### 🚀 About Me

I'm a Computer Science student at **Saarland University** passionate about bridging the gap between low-level systems and modern **applied AI engineering**. I specialize in developing resilient asynchronous backends, production-grade retrieval pipelines, and stateful agentic workflows that produce reliable, hallucination-free outputs.

- 🎓 **Studies:** B.Sc. Computer Science at Saarland University (focus on Software Engineering, Systems, & Data Structures)
- 🧠 **Focus Areas:** Agentic AI architectures, grounded RAG pipelines, AST-level code intelligence, and systems engineering
- 🛠️ **Current Experiments:** Stateful multi-agent graphs with **LangGraph** & **PydanticAI**, LLM tracing with **Langfuse**, and RISC-V internals
- 💬 **Ask me about:** Python, FastAPI, RAG with line-level citations, custom compilers, and PyTorch from scratch
- ⚡ **Fun Fact:** I enjoy building systems from first principles — from custom compilers down to training word embeddings mathematically without high-level abstractions
